# NOVA Browser Privacy Policy & Transparency Manifesto

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026  
**Product:** NOVA Browser  

---

## 1. Summary of Our Commitments

NOVA Browser was created with an absolute commitment to privacy. Our policy is simple:

- **We collect zero personal data.**
- **We collect zero browsing data.**
- **We transmit zero telemetry.**
- **We run zero analytics scripts.**
- **We do not require user accounts or registration.**
- **We do not monetize your browsing activity.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

NOVA Browser does not collect, log, transmit, or share:
- The websites you visit (URLs, domains, paths, or query parameters).
- The terms you type into the address bar or search boxes.
- Your IP address, device identifier, hardware specification, or operating system fingerprint.
- Your bookmarks, saved passwords, or form inputs.
- Your download history or filenames.
- Your session durations, feature clicks, or crash logs.

---

## 3. Data Stored on Your Local Device

All data generated by your usage of NOVA Browser remains strictly on your local machine:
1. **Browsing History:** Stored in `%APPDATA%/nova-browser/nova_data/history.json`. You can disable history recording at any time in Settings, or purge it via "Clear Browsing Data".
2. **Bookmarks:** Stored in `bookmarks.json`. Bookmarks never leave your computer.
3. **Cookies & Website Storage:** Managed by the local Chromium engine. In normal windows, cookies persist until you clear them or the website expires them. In **Private Browsing Windows**, all cookies, cache, and website storage exist only in volatile RAM and are destroyed immediately when the private window is closed.
4. **Permissions:** When you grant or deny a website permission to access your camera, microphone, or location, that choice is stored in `settings.json` and can be revoked at any time.

---

## 4. Network Connections

The only external network requests initiated by or through NOVA Browser are:
1. **Websites You Explicitly Navigate To:** When you enter a URL or search query, NOVA connects to that destination to retrieve and render the page requested by you.
2. **Search Provider Queries:** When you submit a search query, NOVA sends the query to the search engine you selected (DuckDuckGo by default). NOVA never sends keystrokes or predictive search queries to any third party while you are typing.

---

## 5. Privacy Shield (Ad & Tracker Blocker)

NOVA Browser includes an integrated content-blocking shield that inspects outgoing network requests against a local filter list.
- **Local Rule Matching:** Request evaluation occurs 100% locally on your machine.
- **No Remote Telemetry:** The number of trackers or ads blocked on any webpage is tracked only in local application memory to display on your Privacy Shield popup.
- **No Sponsored Whitelisting:** NOVA does not accept payments to whitelist advertisers.

---

## 6. Honest Security & Privacy Boundaries

While NOVA protects you against third-party web trackers and client-side profiling, you should understand the boundaries of browser-level protection:
- **Local Network & ISP Visibility:** Unencrypted DNS lookups and plaintext HTTP requests can be viewed by network operators and ISPs unless you use a VPN or encrypted DNS proxy.
- **First-Party Data Collection:** If you log into a web service (e.g., social networks or email accounts), that service can track what you do within its platform.
- **Malicious Downloads:** While NOVA blocks silent file execution and warns on dangerous file extensions, you should only download and run software from sources you trust.

---

## 7. Open Source & Verifiability

The complete source code of NOVA Browser is available for inspection and auditing. Anyone can verify that no hidden telemetry or unauthorized network requests exist in the application code.
